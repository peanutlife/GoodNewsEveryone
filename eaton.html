One year after the Eaton Fire, Altadena is finding small ways to celebrate the holidays

💡

Why This Matters

In the wake of the Eaton Fire, Altadena's resilience shines through as the community finds joy in small gestures and connections during the holiday season. Each twinkling light on the Postmates delivery truck represents a beacon of hope and togetherness for a town that has persevered through challenges. By coming together to celebrate in creative ways, Altadena shows that even in the face of adversity, the spirit of unity and joy can prevail.
