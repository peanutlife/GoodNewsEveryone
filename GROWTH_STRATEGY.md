## 1. Reddit Distribution Strategy (Highest ROI)

**Why Reddit?** Positive news consistently goes viral on Reddit.

### Target Subreddits:
- **r/UpliftingNews** - 17M members (PRIMARY TARGET)
- **r/MadeMeSmile** - 7M members
- **r/HumansBeingBros** - 4M members
- **r/MadeMeCry** - 1M members (happy tears)
- **r/happy** - 300K members
- **r/GoodNews** - 50K members
- **r/wholesome** - 2M members

### Reddit Posting Strategy:

**Account Setup:**
```
1. Create Reddit account TODAY
2. Comment genuinely in target subreddits (build karma)
3. Wait 1-2 weeks before posting links
4. Post 1-2 articles per week MAX (not spam)
```

**Best Posting Times:**
- **Monday-Thursday: 8-10am ET** (US waking up)
- **Sunday evening: 7-9pm ET** (people winding down)

**Title Formula:**
```
✅ GOOD: "After 15 years in shelter, senior dog finally finds forever home"
❌ BAD: "Dog adoption story - Peanutlife"

✅ GOOD: "Teenager invents device that cleans ocean plastic - and it works"
❌ BAD: "New invention for ocean cleaning"
```

**Expected Results:**
- Average post: 50-200 upvotes = 500-2000 visitors
- Good post: 500-1000 upvotes = 5,000-10,000 visitors
- Viral post: 5000+ upvotes = 50,000-200,000 visitors
- Hit rate: ~10% of posts go semi-viral

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Create Reddit account
- [ ] Subscribe to all target subreddits
- [ ] Comment 2-3 times daily for 2 weeks
- [ ] Identify your top 10 most uplifting articles
- [ ] Post first article (use compelling title)
